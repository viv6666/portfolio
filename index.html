Boards first, then the schedule around them

I started with hardware: firmware on STM32 and ESP32, and custom PCBs in KiCad. At Syrma SGS I’m learning the other half of getting a board built, which is the plan, the parts and the follow-up.

The two sides connect for me. Knowing what a BOM revision or a 4-layer stack-up means makes it easier to ask the right question when material is short or a build slips. My long-term direction is program ownership in manufacturing operations, and I’m building toward it with stronger EMS knowledge, disciplined execution and Excel-based reporting.
