Graduate Engineer Trainee (GET) at Syrma SGS Technology Pvt. Ltd., brought into the Future Bets Program starting July 2026 — an Electronics and Communication Engineering graduate from SRMIST Chennai stepping into the Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) industry.

My grounding spans the full electronics stack — from custom PCB fabrication and embedded firmware to cloud-connected IoT hardware. Now I'm extending that into manufacturing engineering: NPI, DFM/DFA, SMT assembly, and electronics testing at industrial scale.

Fault-First Thinking
Every system I design accounts for failure modes before deployment. Redundancy is not optional — it's architectural.
Design for Manufacture
A board that's hard to build is a board that's wrong. DFM and DFA thinking start at schematic capture, not at the SMT line.
Hardware ↔ Firmware Unity
PCB design and embedded firmware are the same problem. I work both simultaneously to eliminate integration failures at the source.
Quality Is Engineered In
Testing, failure analysis, and continuous improvement aren't a final gate — they're built into the process from the first prototype.
